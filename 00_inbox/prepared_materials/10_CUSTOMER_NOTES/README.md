# Customer Evidence

No customer interviews or usage evidence have been collected in this repository yet.

Do not turn founder enthusiasm, social-media comments, or competitor popularity into fake demand evidence.

## Evidence levels

1. Observed workflow and direct quotation with permission
2. Recorded interview notes
3. Customer-provided artifact or process map
4. Commitment to test
5. Active prototype usage
6. Payment or signed design partnership
7. Retention and repeated use

## Initial interview targets

Recruit across at least two candidate segments before choosing the wedge:

- technical founders and small software teams
- research teams or laboratories
- data and analytics professionals
- operations-heavy small businesses
- professional-services teams

## What to capture

- actual recurring job
- trigger and frequency
- current tools
- current workaround
- time and labor cost
- failure cost
- approval and security boundaries
- what users will not delegate
- required integrations
- objective completion state
- willingness to test
- willingness to pay
- procurement constraints

Use `INTERVIEW_TEMPLATE.md`. Store recordings only with permission and never commit sensitive customer data to this public repository.
