## Objective

Which customer-workflow hypothesis is this interview testing?

## Questions

1. Walk me through the last time you did this work.
2. What triggered it?
3. Which tools and people were involved?
4. Where did you copy, reconcile, or re-enter information?
5. What usually goes wrong?
6. How do you know the work is actually complete?
7. What parts require judgment or approval?
8. What information is too sensitive to delegate?
9. How often does this happen?
10. What does it cost in time, delay, errors, or missed opportunity?
11. What have you tried already?
12. Why did the current solution fail or remain incomplete?
13. Which result would make a pilot worthwhile?
14. What would prevent you from installing or paying for this product?
15. Who decides whether to buy it?

Do not begin by describing the full product. Ask about past behavior before hypothetical interest.
