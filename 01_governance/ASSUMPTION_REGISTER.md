# Assumption Register

| ID | Assumption | Impact if wrong | Evidence needed | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-001 | Technical founders and small teams are the best initial segment. | Wrong product and sales motion. | 15 to 25 interviews across candidate segments and pilot commitments. | OPEN |
| A-002 | A narrow persistent engineering or research workflow creates enough value to pay. | Prototype has no commercial wedge. | Time, failure-cost, and willingness-to-pay evidence. | OPEN |
| A-003 | One reliable visible agent is more valuable initially than a visible agent team. | MVP sequencing may be wrong. | User tests and workflow decomposition. | OPEN |
| A-004 | Multi-model orchestration improves outcomes enough to justify complexity. | Higher cost and fragility without benefit. | Single-model versus multi-model benchmark. | OPEN |
| A-005 | Verified completion is a meaningful purchasing advantage. | Differentiation is weak. | Customer interviews and completion-trust experiments. | OPEN |
| A-006 | A web control plane is enough for the first prototype. | Native client becomes critical path. | Workflow and takeover requirements. | OPEN |
| A-007 | Initial experiments can remain near USD 0 to 100 monthly. | Budget and architecture need revision. | Spike cost data. | OPEN |
| A-008 | Persistent workspace plus isolated task sandboxes can balance continuity and security. | Major architecture change. | Compute and credential spikes. | OPEN |
| A-009 | Publicly cataloged provider APIs will be available to the founder's funded accounts and will support the required tool, context, and structured-output behavior in practice. | Routing design fails or benchmark candidates cannot run. | Account model listing or minimal authorized call, account limits, and the project benchmark. | OPEN |
| A-010 | Customers will accept cloud execution for the target workflow. | Deployment model changes. | Security and procurement interviews. | OPEN |
| A-011 | The current official Grok Bot documentation is materially representative of behavior on an eligible target account. | The parity baseline overstates available or working behavior. | Record build, plan, region, rollout, and repeated black-box tests on an authorized account. | OPEN |
| A-012 | The documented Grok Bot capabilities are reliable enough on representative workflows to be a useful functional benchmark. | Parity priorities optimize for documented breadth rather than dependable outcomes. | Repeated workflow runs measuring verified success, false completion, intervention, recovery, latency, and limits. | OPEN |
| A-013 | The initial benchmark can use scrubbed or synthetic fixtures without losing the task characteristics that distinguish candidate models. | Model selection will not generalize to real project or customer work. | Compare fixture performance with later authorized real-workflow results and revise the suite. | OPEN |
