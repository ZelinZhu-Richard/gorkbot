# Assumption Register

## Confirmed D-016 operating context (not assumptions)

While D-016 engineering-first mode is active, startup/customer execution is deferred and the Engineering Preview is active. The first product target is a local-first, single-user persistent software-engineering agent. Architecture remains unselected. E3 is a technical-spike and model-role-benchmark gate: independent E3 verification may establish `AUTONOMY_ELIGIBLE`, but it cannot authorize implementation. Autonomous build remains `NOT_AUTHORIZED` until a separate explicit founder decision records `AUTONOMOUS_BUILD_AUTHORIZED`. Future cloud and multi-tenant SaaS remain compatibility constraints, not v0.1 implementation requirements. Existing startup assumptions and their evidence needs remain preserved, but their execution is deferred while D-016 is active.
